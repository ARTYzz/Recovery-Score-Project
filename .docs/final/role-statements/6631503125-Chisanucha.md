## Reflection

Working as the Tech Lead of the this project taught me how AI can change the software development process. Throughout the project, I used AI to explore technical solutions, plan system architecture, understand unfamiliar technologies, and solve coding problems. One major improvement was development speed. AI helped us understand complex features, such as Apple HealthKit integration and the recovery scoring system, without spending too much time researching everything from scratch. However, I also learned that AI-generated code and suggestions are not always reliable. For example, we discovered limitations when retrieving HRV data from certain smartwatches through Apple HealthKit. This experience taught me that technical decisions must be based on actual testing rather than AI suggestions alone. As a Tech Lead, I also learned the importance of maintaining code quality, protecting sensitive health data, communicating with teammates, and ensuring that everyone understands the system we are building.

This project made me realize that I still have a lot to learn as a developer. Although AI makes programming more accessible, I believe understanding the fundamentals of software engineering is still essential. I see myself as someone who can learn new technologies, solve problems, and take responsibility for technical decisions, but I still need to strengthen my coding and system design skills.

Over the next year, I plan to improve my programming skills, especially Python and SQL, while learning more about cloud computing, databases, and infrastructure. I also want to gain experience building and deploying real-world applications. In five years, I hope to become a skilled engineer who can design reliable systems, lead technical projects, and make informed decisions independently. My goal is not just to become better at using AI, but to become an engineer who truly understands what AI helps me build.

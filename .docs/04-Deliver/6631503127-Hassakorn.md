Ownership and Contributions

During the Boxer Recovery App project, I owned the  execution, and data analysis of the project's evaluation surveys. Because our current testing phase relied on simulated user testing rather than real professional fighters, gathering precise, structured feedback was critical. I led the deployment of the System Usability Scale (SUS) and Technology Acceptance Model (TAM) surveys to rigorously evaluate our prototype's usability and overall value proposition.

My primary responsibility was processing the raw survey responses to calculate our final SUS and TAM scores. I analyzed this data to validate if our 90-second daily check-in loop was effective and to identify major UX friction points. I then synthesized these quantitative and qualitative findings into the project Case Study, highlighting key areas for improvement—specifically the need for UI icons, better localization, and a user request for AI-driven guidance. Additionally, I helped format the comprehensive Test Plan that guided our testing scenarios.

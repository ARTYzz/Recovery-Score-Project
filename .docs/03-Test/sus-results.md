# SUS and Acceptance Survey — PROXY Results

> **PROXY.** The 8 respondents are the same friends who did the [session tasks](session-logs.md): outside the team, **not** professional boxers in fight camp. Their answers about usability are meaningful; their answers about usefulness in a fight camp ("would improve my fight camp", "would use every day of my camp") are opinions of non-boxers and should be read with caution.

- **Instrument:** Google Form "Recovery Score", filled in after the session tasks.
- **Source data:** Google Sheet "Recovery Score (Responses)", sheet "Form Responses 1", rows 2–9 (team Google Drive). Timestamps are omitted here.
- **Responses:** 8, all dated 17 Nov 2026, all gave PDPA consent, all role "Athlete" as selected in the form.
- **Linking:** survey rows S1–S8 are in submission order. They are **not** linked one-to-one to PROXY-01 – PROXY-08 in the session log.

**Benchmark:** the commonly used SUS average is 68 (Sauro, *A Practical Guide to the System Usability Scale*, 2011; <https://measuringu.com/sus/>). Our PROXY mean of 56.9 is **below average**. Only 2 of 8 respondents (S2, S4) scored above 68.

⚠ **Data quality:** S1 answered 4 to every item and S3 answered 5 to every item. Because SUS mixes positive and negative statements, this gives contradictory answers (e.g. "easy to use" = 5 and "unnecessarily complex" = 5). These rows are kept but likely reflect careless answering; the right-hand column shows the result without them. Either way the score stays below 68.

### What SUS says about the app

- Q4 "I would need the support of a technical person" mean 4.0 — most respondents agreed they would need help.
- Q10 "needed to learn a lot before I could get going" mean 4.13 — high. This matches the open comments asking for a tutorial (section 3) and the T5 errors in the session log.
